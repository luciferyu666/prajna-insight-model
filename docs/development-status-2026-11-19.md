## Research direction

The D05 development route separates three stages: Phase A studies an insight-oriented system around existing model or scripted components; Phase B proposes derived weights through authorized post-training; Phase C proposes weights trained from random initialization. Neither Phase B nor Phase C is established by these Foundation results. No new model training or scientific breakthrough is claimed here.

The near-term priority is to resolve source rights, access and data-handling review, reconcile execution evidence, and obtain the applicable scoped acceptance decisions. Subsequent verifier, adapter, baseline and experiment packages remain subject to their own requirements and authorization.

## Distinguish checks from stronger guarantees

- A finite contract check concerns the declared finite domain and verifier. It does not by itself validate the original human intent.
- Replay consistency concerns a recorded execution contract. It does not establish task success, oracle correctness, independent replication or generalization.
- A hash identifies observed bytes. It does not enforce immutable storage or atomic commitment.
- A proposal status PROPOSED is not confirmation, adoption or permission to replace a fixed parent contract.
- A successful demonstration process may contain expected FAIL and UNKNOWN fields. Those fields must remain visible.
- A missing resource-release receipt leaves release UNKNOWN; it does not prove zero leakage or establish a leak.

General Specification Fidelity, an implemented Intent Reassessment loop, eight-component state sufficiency/Markov properties, optimal or learned CAIG, Atomic Snapshot Commit and External Audit Journal are not established by this candidate. WP04 runtime reliability limitations remain unresolved. Local engineering evidence does not establish superior reasoning performance or close human scientific/publication review.

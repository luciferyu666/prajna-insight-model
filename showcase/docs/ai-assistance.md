# AI Assistance and Research Provenance

Human and AI contributions must be distinguished from complete provenance, scientific proof and authorship responsibility.

## Known activity-level contributions

- Human participation includes research direction, recorded bounded scientific reviews and architecture decisions. This does not imply independent human derivation or verification of every result.
- ChatGPT assisted early architecture and mathematical discussions, research specifications and preparation of Codex work packages.
- Codex participated extensively in candidate formalization, software development and testing, experimental/statistical analysis, literature checking, auditing, and manuscript/document preparation.
- The complete provenance of individual formulas, passages and some research decisions remains PARTIAL or UNKNOWN. Missing logs do not mean no AI participation.

These statements describe known activities, not formula-by-formula attribution or an assertion that AI originated every idea. No complete historical ChatGPT log is claimed.

## Review and responsibility

Separate AI Reviewer/Validator contexts provide bounded engineering review. They are not external human scientific peer review. Full human scientific review remains incomplete; no human signature, comprehensive author responsibility or journal eligibility is inferred.

AI tools are not human authors. No statement here implies OpenAI endorsement, confirmed funding, absence of conflicts, cleared third-party rights or publication approval.

This local documentation does not modify the sealed manuscript or its previously reviewed AI disclosure. It supplies no named author/contact/affiliation metadata and grants no software or publication license. Public redistribution rights and software licensing remain uncleared.
